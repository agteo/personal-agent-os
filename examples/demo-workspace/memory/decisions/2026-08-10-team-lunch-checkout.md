# Show Timing Before Payment

## Date

2026-08-10

## Decision

The checkout flow should show estimated pickup or delivery time before payment.

## Context

Interviewees said uncertainty about timing was one of the biggest causes of frustration during busy workdays.

## Options Considered

- Show timing on restaurant cards.
- Show timing during checkout.
- Send timing only after payment.

## Rationale

Showing timing during checkout gives people enough certainty before committing without overcrowding browsing screens.

## Consequences

- Prototype needs a clear timing component.
- Research should test whether users notice the timing before paying.
