# Show Pickup Time Before Payment

## Date

2026-08-10

## Decision

The checkout flow should show estimated pickup time before payment.

## Context

Students said uncertainty about pickup timing was one of the biggest causes of frustration during lunch rush.

## Options Considered

- Show pickup time on restaurant cards.
- Show pickup time during checkout.
- Send pickup time only after payment.

## Rationale

Showing pickup time during checkout gives students enough certainty before committing without overcrowding browsing screens.

## Consequences

- Prototype needs a clear pickup-time component.
- Research should test whether students notice the timing before paying.
